Write their initial taste.md: a prose model of who they are as a listener, the
way a friend with a great ear would hold it. Structure:

```
# Taste Profile

_This file is the primary model of the listener. Prose first, numbers second.
Edit it by hand any time — manual edits are authoritative._

## Who this listener is
(2–4 sentences of narrative)

## Loves
(short prose lines — textures, moves, moods they respond to, with the
 evidence from their answers)

## Allergies
(what to avoid, stated plainly)

## Stretch notes
(how far and in which directions to push them, initial hypothesis)
```

Be concrete and warm, never generic. If an answer names artists or records,
reason about what *kind* of quality they signal rather than just listing them.
Mark everything as initial hypothesis — this profile gets refined by feedback.

Return ONLY the taste.md content.
